Who we are

私たちについて

Friends and colleagues, building software from Fujisawa.

We’re Topis Mos, a team of Japanese software engineers based in Fujisawa, Japan. We’re friends as well as colleagues, and we hold bachelor’s and master’s degrees in computer science from the Shonan Institute of Technology.

Each of us is a senior engineer with more than seven years of experience. Our core is backend engineering and AI, and we work across the whole stack when a project needs it.

We also do AI training work: creating, reviewing and evaluating the data used to train and test large language models. That work keeps us close to how models actually behave.

02

Our way

大切にしていること

Technology matters.
The people behind it matter more.

Four ideas from Japanese working culture shape how we work.

  • ものづくり

    Monozukuri Craftsmanship

    Pride in making things well, including the parts nobody sees: clear data models, tests, and code the next engineer can read.

  • 改善

    Kaizen Continuous improvement

    Small, steady improvements over big rewrites. Every release should leave the product a little better than the last.

  • 報連相

    Hō-ren-sō Report, contact, consult

    Regular updates, early warnings and shared decisions, so nothing is a surprise across time zones.

  • おもてなし

    Omotenashi Anticipating needs

    Thinking ahead for the people we work with, and for the people who will use what we build.
