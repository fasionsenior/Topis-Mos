Who we are

We’re Topis Mos, a team of Japanese software engineers based in Kamakura, Japan. We studied computer science together at the Shonan Institute of Technology, earning our bachelor’s and master’s degrees, and we’ve worked side by side as friends and colleagues ever since.

We’re senior engineers with more than seven years of experience shipping production software. Our core is backend engineering and AI, and we work across the stack when a project needs it.

  • Backend development
  • AI and LLM applications
  • Frontend development
  • Full-stack products
  • Blockchain development
  • AI training data and evaluation

We also work on AI training: creating, reviewing and evaluating the data used to train and test large language models on platforms such as Snorkel, Handshake, micro1 and DataAnnotation. That work keeps us close to how models actually behave, which shapes how we build AI products for clients.

Tokyo, San Francisco, New York

Our Tokyo team overlaps with US West Coast business hours early each morning, Pacific time.
